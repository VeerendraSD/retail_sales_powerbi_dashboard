# Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and operational trends.

## Project Overview

This project transforms retail sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business KPIs and enables users to analyze performance across time, product categories, regions, customer segments, and shipping modes.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance
- Track revenue and profit trends over time
- Identify high-performing regions and product categories
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Analyze order distribution across shipping modes
- Identify top-performing products by revenue
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPI cards, slicers, and dashboards

## Dashboard Features

### Sales & Revenue Analysis
- Revenue trends by year
- Revenue performance by product category
- Monthly revenue analysis
- Top 5 products by revenue

### Profitability Analysis
- Profit by region
- Profit by product category
- Overall profit margin tracking

### Customer & Order Analysis
- Revenue contribution by customer segment
- Order distribution by shipping mode
- Interactive year and category filters

## Key Insights

- Generated **2.33M in total revenue** with **292.30K in total profit**.
- Overall profit margin stands at **12.56%**.
- The dashboard tracks **5,111 orders across 804 customers**.
- Technology is the highest-revenue product category.
- Regional analysis highlights differences in profitability across West, East, South, and Central regions.
- Customer segment and shipping-mode analysis provide additional insights into purchasing and order patterns.
